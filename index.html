What to look for

These signs can have other causes. AI cannot confirm an infestation from one photo.

🕳️
D-shaped exit holes

Small D-shaped holes in the bark may be a warning sign.

🪵
Splitting bark

Look for unusual cracks or bark pulling away from the trunk.

🍃
Thinning crown

Leaves and branches may thin out, but a full-tree photo is needed to assess this.
